# Placement review — 2026-09-23

> **Decision (Carl, 2026-09-23): no placement changes.** Placements are judged on scheduled
> appointments, not cost per lead; a high-CPL placement is fine if its leads book. GoHighLevel
> bookings carry no placement today, so this CPL-based review is kept for reference only.

Scope: every client's ACTIVE lead/conversion campaigns (boosted posts and legacy page promotions
excluded). Source: Meta insights with `breakdowns=publisher_platform,platform_position`,
last 90 days (with the last 30 days alongside). "Leads" = Meta-reported leads (Instant Form +
pixel Lead), the same definition as the dashboard. Nothing was changed; this is a proposal.

## How to read it

- A placement is flagged **CUT** when it spent at least one account-average lead's worth
  (minimum $40) with zero leads, and **REVIEW** when its cost per lead is 2x the account's
  or worse with at least 5% of spend.
- Placement-level numbers are Meta leads only. GoHighLevel bookings carry no placement, so
  "which placement books appointments" cannot be measured; lead cost is the best proxy.
- Most accounts have single-digit leads per placement. Treat small numbers as noise.
- Placement edits are **ad set** edits and restart the learning phase. US Pro's newest
  campaigns (17–20 Sep) are still learning; editing them now resets that again.
